# Video Script Templates

Work through it in this order: **test the product → fill in the test notes → generate scripts from the notes → film → run the pre-post checklist in `STRATEGY.md` → post.**

Every script is written from what you actually saw, so each video's claims come from your own test.

---

## Step 1: Test notes (fill one in per product)

Copy this into a note on your phone while you test.

```
PRODUCT:            (exact listing name)
PRICE / COMMISSION: $__ / __%
HOW I GOT IT:       bought it myself / free sample from seller
DAYS TESTED:        __

THE PROBLEM IT CLAIMS TO SOLVE:
WHAT I TRIED IT ON:          (specific: "grease on the stovetop gap", not "cleaning")
WHAT ACTUALLY HAPPENED:      (be specific and honest)
BEST MOMENT ON CAMERA:       (the 2-second visual that proves it)
WHAT DISAPPOINTED ME:
WHO SHOULD NOT BUY THIS:
WOULD I BUY IT AGAIN?        yes / no / only if ___
QUESTIONS PEOPLE WILL ASK:   (size? does it work on X? how long does it last?)
```

If "What actually happened" is weak, **don't make a shoppable video.** Make an "I tried the viral X so you don't have to" video instead. It builds trust, and trust is what sells the next product.

## Step 2: Generate scripts with Claude

Paste this prompt into Claude with your filled-in test notes:

```
You are writing short TikTok Shop affiliate scripts. Rules:
- Use ONLY experiences and results stated in my test notes. Do not invent results,
  durations, comparisons, or claims. If something is missing, say so instead of guessing.
- Write in first person as me, casual, like talking to a friend. No hype words like
  "game-changer" or "obsessed".
- Include a spoken or on-screen disclosure line (I earn commission / #ad if a free sample).
- No medical or health claims.
- Hook must show or name the PROBLEM in the first 2 seconds, not the product.
- 15–35 seconds when read aloud.

For each of these 3 formats, give: HOOK (spoken + on-screen text), SHOT LIST with
timings, VOICEOVER, ON-SCREEN TEXT, CAPTION (under 150 chars), 3–5 HASHTAGS:
  1. Problem → satisfying fix
  2. Honest review: who it's NOT for
  3. [pick the best fit: side-by-side test / stress test / reply to comment]

Then give me 5 alternative hooks I can A/B test.

MY TEST NOTES:
<paste here>
```

## Step 3: The six formats

Timing guide for every format: **0–2s hook · 2–15s proof · 15–25s caveat or objection · last 3s CTA and disclosure.**

### 1. Problem → satisfying fix
Best for: cleaning tools, organizers, kitchen gadgets.

| Time | Shot | On-screen text |
|---|---|---|
| 0–2s | Close-up of the **problem** (grime, mess, tangle) | "This was driving me crazy" |
| 2–4s | The product comes into frame (hands only) | "$__ fix?" |
| 4–15s | Real-time or lightly sped-up fix, uncut if possible | Brief step labels |
| 15–22s | Before/after, side by side | "Before / After" |
| 22–27s | One honest caveat | "Heads up: ___" |
| 27–30s | Point at the product link | "Linked 🛒 · I earn commission" |

Hook bank:
- "If your ___ looks like this, watch the next 10 seconds."
- "I didn't think this would work on ___."
- "POV: you finally deal with the ___."

### 2. Honest review: who it's NOT for
Best for: anything. Builds trust and converts well.

| Time | Shot | On-screen text |
|---|---|---|
| 0–2s | Product in hand, slightly skeptical | "Don't buy this if…" |
| 2–10s | Show the dealbreaker(s) honestly | "❌ you have ___" / "❌ you want ___" |
| 10–22s | Show what it's great at, with a real demo | "✅ BUT if you ___" |
| 22–30s | Verdict | "Would I rebuy? ___" + disclosure |

Hook bank:
- "Don't buy the viral ___ until you see this."
- "3 reasons NOT to buy this, and 1 reason I kept it."
- "Honest review after __ days."

### 3. Side-by-side test
Best for: showing it beats the old way or a pricier option.

| Time | Shot | On-screen text |
|---|---|---|
| 0–2s | Split screen of both options | "$__ vs $__" or "Old way vs this" |
| 2–18s | Same task, same conditions, both sides | A timer on screen |
| 18–25s | Result | "Winner: ___" (be honest, even if it lost) |
| 25–30s | CTA | "Linked · commission" |

Only compare things you actually ran through the same test.

### 4. Stress or durability test
Best for: containers, cables, bags, anything that claims to be "unbreakable" or "leakproof."

- Hook: "They say it's ___. Let's see."
- Escalate the test in 3 steps (normal → tough → extreme).
- End on the real result, including failure. Failures often get more views.

### 5. "Things I'd buy again under $25"
Best for: once you've tested 3 or more products. Links go to your showcase.

- One product every 5–7 seconds. Each gets its best 2-second proof clip plus one line of why.
- Include only products you tested and would genuinely rebuy.
- Disclosure: "All linked, and I earn commission on these."

### 6. Reply to a comment
Best for: turning questions into videos and showing the algorithm people are engaging.

- Use TikTok's "reply with video" on a real comment. The comment sticker is the hook.
- Answer with a demo, not talk ("Does it work on glass? Let's try it").
- These tend to convert well because the viewer's objection is already on screen.

## Filming kit (phone only)

- **Light:** face a window, or use a $15–$25 clip-on light. Lighting matters more than the camera.
- **Stability:** a phone tripod or a stack of books. Shoot top-down for hands-only demos.
- **Sound:** the phone mic within about 2 feet is fine. Record voiceover in a closet if the room echoes.
- **Framing:** 9:16 vertical. Keep text and the product away from the bottom 20% and right 15%, where TikTok's buttons and caption sit.
- **Editing:** use CapCut. Turn on auto-captions, cut dead air to 0 frames, and keep the first frame visually busy.

## Labeling and disclosure

| You used… | Do this |
|---|---|
| A free sample or commission link | Spoken or on-screen disclosure, and TikTok's content-disclosure toggle on |
| An AI voiceover (e.g. CapCut text-to-speech) | Turn on the "AI-generated content" label |
| AI-generated background, B-roll or images | Turn on the AI-generated content label. **Never** use AI to show the product itself working. |
| Only real footage and your own voice | No AI label needed. Disclosure is still needed if it's an affiliate video. |
